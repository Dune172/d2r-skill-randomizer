### 1. Blizzard D2R Forums — Mechanics questions in "High Stakes" thread
**Link:** https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/d2r/t/d2r-randomiser-challenge/176783  
**Posted:** Jun 20–22, 2026 (3 replies)  
**Context:** User Onadroig-1677 shared Challenge 8 on the official forums. User TrangOul-1537 followed up with two real questions: (1) how do you get normal items for runewords if House Always Wins blocks weapon/armor drops? (2) what does "+50% elemental damage" do for skills that don't deal elemental damage?

**Suggested reply** (as dev, reply to TrangOul-1537):

> Good questions — I'm the dev.
>
> On runewords: base items (white/grey weapons and armor) can still drop from racks, chest pops, and superunique kills — House Always Wins only blocks monster drop tables and vendor stock. So you can still find eth colossus blades on the ground; you just can't buy a Monarch. Gambling covers magic and rare slots, but white bases are still out there. It does make specific runewords harder to set up, which is intentional.
>
> On the elemental damage bonus from Arcane Surge: it only applies to the elemental component of skills that deal it. Skills with zero elemental damage see no benefit from that part of the mutation — the mana cost increase still applies either way. If you rolled a physical-heavy shuffle, Arcane Surge is mostly just a tax. That tension is part of what makes each seed play differently with the same mutation set.
>
> Challenge 9 drops today if you want to give it a run — https://d2rrandomizer.com/challenge?utm_source=blizzforums

**Reasoning:** TrangOul asked specific, on-topic mechanics questions. A precise dev answer adds genuine value, reads as helpful rather than promotional, and the forum is general discussion (no self-promo rule). The link is contextually appropriate since Challenge 9 starts today. Discloses dev relationship in the first line.
