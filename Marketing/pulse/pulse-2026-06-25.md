**Suggested reply** (post as the dev, answering TrangOul's questions directly):

> Hey, I'm the dev behind this. Good questions about High Stakes. Runewords: rune and gem drops from monsters are unaffected — only weapon and armor drops are replaced with gold. You can still cube runes and find socketed bases through gambling or chests, so runewords are harder to build but not impossible. That's intentional; High Stakes is a "gamblers only" economy, not a runewords-off challenge.
>
> On "+50% elemental damage" — the mutation boosts the elemental component of skills that have one. Pure physical skills and utility skills (buffs, curses, summons) are unaffected. So Frozen Orb hits harder; Whirlwind does not. It's a trade-off based on what your randomized tree landed on.
>
> Ashgar's point about SSF HC is fair. The randomizer isn't for everyone. If you want "sane" D2, you can run any seed without mutations — that's already a clean challenge-run format. The mutations are the chaos layer, and they're all opt-in.
>
> (Disclosure: I built d2rrandomizer.com)
